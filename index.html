Make short videos. Review them. Publish where your audience is.

Fábrica de Vídeos is a desktop app for content creators. It turns an idea, a script, photos or a long video into finished vertical videos (9:16), and publishes each one to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts — only after the creator reviews and approves it.

Features

Clips from long videos

Finds the best moments of a long video and cuts them into short vertical clips with subtitles.

Videos from a script

The creator writes the text; the app splits it into scenes, creates the images and records the narration.

Videos from photos

Turns a set of photos into a narrated video, with gentle camera motion or AI animation.

Short series

Plans and produces short story episodes with recurring characters and consistent voices.

Review before publishing

Every video is shown to the creator first. Nothing is posted without a manual "Publish" click.

One place to publish

Connect TikTok, YouTube and Instagram once, then publish an approved video to the chosen accounts.

How it works

  1. Create a project — choose the type (clips, script, photos or series) and add your material.
  2. The app produces the video — on the creator's own computer, using local AI models.
  3. Review — watch the result, approve it or ask for changes.
  4. Publish — pick the accounts and settings and click Publish.

Publishing to TikTok

Fábrica de Vídeos uses TikTok Login Kit so the creator can connect their own TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to upload and post the videos they approved. Before each post the app shows:

  • the name of the TikTok account the video will be posted to;
  • "Who can view this video", with only the privacy options available for that account (no option pre-selected);
  • Allow comments, Duet and Stitch (off by default, and disabled when the account has turned them off);
  • commercial content disclosure (Your brand / Branded content);
  • TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and, when applicable, Branded Content Policy.

Permissions requested: user.info.basic (show which account is connected), video.upload and video.publish (post the approved video). The app does not read followers, messages, other users' data or analytics.

Your data

Access tokens, basic account info (display name and ID) and the videos stay on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties; a video is sent only to the platform the creator chose when publishing. Access can be revoked at any time in the authorized apps settings of TikTok, Google and Instagram. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Contact

Questions about the app or your data: send a direct message to the channel's TikTok account.
